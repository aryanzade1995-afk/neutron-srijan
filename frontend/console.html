These are ranking figures on synthetic behaviour, not a performance claim. Mule accounts here are recruited the way real rings recruit them — 35% freshly opened, 50% existing accounts turned, 15% dormant accounts reactivated — so account age does not give the model a free answer, and the scores are correspondingly modest. The chain walk is the deterministic part that transfers to real data; this scorer only orders the queue, and would need retraining on NPCI's own graph using the verdicts captured under Feedback.
