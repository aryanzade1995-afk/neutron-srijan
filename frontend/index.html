One flagged transaction in. One account to freeze out.

Accounts are nodes, transactions are time-stamped edges. The walk only continues where the money plausibly went — after it arrived, fast, and mostly intact.

1

Ingest

Every transfer lands as a directed edge carrying amount, timestamp and mode — P2P, P2M or cash-out.

2

Trigger

A victim complaint names a transaction, or the classifier flags an account on its own before any complaint exists.

3

Walk forward

Follow outgoing edges that happen after the money arrived, inside the window, forwarding ≥70% — splits included.

4

Name the end node

Where the money stops moving is where it still sits. That account gets the freeze recommendation.
